📖 Quick Glossary
VaR (Value at Risk) — The maximum amount you could lose on a bad day.
  • Historical VaR 95%: On 95% of days, your loss won't exceed this number.
  • Historical VaR 99%: Loss exceeds this only 1 in 100 days.
  • CVaR / Expected Shortfall: If a bad day happens, this is the average loss you'd face.
Parametric VaR — Same idea but calculated using a mathematical formula (assumes normal distribution).
Monte Carlo VaR — Uses thousands of simulated future scenarios to estimate worst-case 30-day loss.
Sharpe Ratio — Return earned per unit of risk. Higher = better. Above 1 is good; above 2 is excellent.
Max Drawdown — The biggest peak-to-valley drop your portfolio has experienced.

📖 GARCH Volatility Table
GARCH Vol (Ann %) — Annualised volatility estimate. E.g., 25% means the stock could swing ±25% in a year.
RAF (Risk Adjustment Factor) — Adjusts portfolio allocation based on risk. Lower RAF = less weight due to higher risk.
Beta — Sensitivity to overall market. Beta >1 = more volatile than market; <1 = calmer.
D/E Ratio — Debt-to-Equity. High D/E = more debt = more financial risk.

🏥 Financial Health KPIs
📖 What is a KPI?

KPI (Key Performance Indicator) — a measurable number that grades a company's financial health. Every KPI comes from one of three financial documents:

  • Income Statement — revenue and expenses over a period
  • Balance Sheet — snapshot of assets vs. liabilities
  • Cash Flow Statement — tracks actual cash moving in/out
Quick Abbreviations: EBITDA · ROE · ROA · P/E · P/B · EV/EBITDA · PEG · OCF · FCF · D/E
